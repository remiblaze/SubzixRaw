# Subzix Raw: one-knob sub-bass weight

![Subzix Raw free one-knob sub-bass weight UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/SubzixRaw/main/subzixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Bigger, deeper low end, level-matched so it gets heavier, never louder.**

Subzix Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Subzix**. Turn up the single **SUB** macro and it adds weight and depth to whatever bass is already there. Great on basslines, kicks, synths, and full mixes.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/SubzixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`SubzixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Subzix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`SubzixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Subzix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **SUB** knob. As you turn it up, Subzix Raw tracks the pitch of your bass and generates a phase-aligned octave-down sub-harmonic, adds a touch of warm low-end saturation and a hint of stereo width up top, while trimming the gain underneath so the low end gets *bigger* without getting louder. At zero it's fully transparent (true bypass is handled by your host). Latency stays fixed no matter how you set it.

That's the whole plugin: one knob, no menus, no setup.
